### 🙋‍♂️ About Me

- 🤖 I evaluate **GenAI systems** — LLMs, RAG pipelines and AI agents — for accuracy, consistency and risk
- 🧪 Bringing years of **software quality engineering** discipline (CI/CD, regression, test frameworks) to AI
- 🏦 Focused on AI in **regulated industries** like banking and financial services
- 🛡️ Interested in **AI governance**, model risk and evidence-based release decisions
- 🌱 Currently building: LLM evaluation and red-teaming frameworks (see below)

### 🚧 What I'm Building

| Project | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Banking RAG Evaluation Suite** | Q&A assistant over banking documents, scored for faithfulness, hallucination and relevance, with regression gates in CI | 🚧 In progress |
| **LLM Red-Team Suite** | OWASP LLM Top 10 attacks on a banking assistant — prompt injection, data leakage, jailbreaks — with risk ratings | 📝 Planned |
